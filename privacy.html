OUTGUESS: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

OUTGUESS is a single-player game. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no login, and it does not send your personal data, gameplay or device information to us or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

Your progress (stars, coins, settings, best score, and the AI's memory of how you play) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes it. The Android app turns off cloud backup, so this data is not copied to Google backups.

The "AI"

The AI opponent is a small pattern-learning model that runs entirely on your device. It learns only from the moves you make in the game and stores what it learned in local storage, as described above.

Ads and game portals

The mobile apps currently show no ads. When you play OUTGUESS on a game website (for example CrazyGames or itch.io), that website may show ads or offer its own account features under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
